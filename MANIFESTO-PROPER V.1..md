# PREFACE
We want this manifesto to be based on love, cooperation, and choice. We do not want this manifesto to be based on hate, competition, and greed. We feel like the systems of power in place, while everchanging and complex, are both able to be defeated and able to be reconstructed, so we may thrive as a more artistic and fair humanity. But in order to both dismantle and replace the systems of power, we must first define and recognize what these systems are, and how they came to be.

# ON COOPERATION WITH THE STATE 
Various anarchists and horizontalists of the past have tried and failed to cooperate with the government of any country. Historically (Such as the anarchists of the CNT/FAI and the anarchists of the Russian Revolution), various older anarchists would participate and join the government, becoming politicians and leaders. This is called absorbtion, and in the context of a power structure which essentially absorbs a person into said system, making the person think that they are doing right by participating in their own oppression, which is counterproductive. The only way that a horizontalist would participate in a government would be through voting. In dire circumstances (Such as the one we live in currently), as well as early in the process of the revolution, voting would not only be viable, but also necessary! Only once, the revolution has snowballed into a society within itself would we try to slowly stop the participation of government, with the logic being that since the power has been replaced/taken away, you would already have the power to change your own lives, and the autonomy to choose your own direction, the participation in government would be unnecessary. Keep in mind, if you choose to vote, you must also be careful with the participation of government. Be wary of the government, and prepare to be backstabbed at any moment. The government is not to be trusted, and will try to subdue your movements to serve their own interests and maintain their own power, at least any sensible government would. Especially when it comes to action, always lean towards direct action. It is extremely important that voting is done, at least in the earlier stages, and you should try to do direct action where you can.

# PILLARS OF THE CURRENT WORLD, INTRO
Our worlds is not black and white. It is a spectrum of identities, power dynamics, and nuances. These nuances, which define our worlds, are also what drives change, and drives the inherent entropy of our world. Yet from this entropy, patterns still emerge. These patterns, specifically in our current human society, are what we call our "Pillars." These pillars support our current ways of life, and make our world of entropy make sense. Keep in mind that I do not speak for the entirety of humanity. These are simply patterns that I, and many other people, have seen and recognized, which have led us to the current world view that we have. 

There are 3 main pillars of our current world. These 3 pillars have different roles to play in our society, and have not always been there. The oldest of these pillars, Prejudice, takes a social and psychological role in our societies, and it connects and plays out in tandem with the other pillars, whatever these other pillars are. The twin of Prejudice, Hierarchy, is more so the motivation and the structure of our society. It is a type of power structure, and it is a power structure which uses domination, authority, obedience, and subservience to accomplish its goals of order and structure, as well as the maintenance of power. The third pillar, the youngest of them all, is Capitalism. It is the economic and final pillar, which is in charge of the distribution, consumption, and production of everything humanity uses. 

Power itself, the driver and fuel of our societies, has always existed, and will continue to exist. Power, in order to maintain itself and keep itself from falling into entropy, creates a structure. People can either follow this power structure willingly, be forced into this power structure, or not participate and create a new power structure. The power structures that you are a part of are usually described as either vertical (Hierarchical) or horizontal (Anarchical). For most of humanity's existence, it is unknown what type of power structure, if there was any. But around 10,000 years ago, give or take, one of the first settler agricultural "civilizations" came to be: Mesopotamia. 

Mesopotamia had a monarchical system, in which a king (Who at first, was also a religious leader) had to take care of his people, just how like a father in a Mesopotamian family should take care of his household. The family, including both the extended and close family, was the base structure for the Mesopotamian government. The duty of the people, according to the Mesopotamian power structure, was to serve the gods, and in turn, the gods serve the people through the government structure. The first kings came to be through the concept of the "Strong Man", which was a kingship based on the skills you had and your ability to lead militarily. Then, kingship was reasoned to be chosen by the gods, and that kingship/the reigning family was chosen by the gods to rule over the people.
