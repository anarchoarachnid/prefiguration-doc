1. `TAGS` ON THE ==OBSIDIAN SYSTEM== START WITH A $HASH$ (#). THE PERCEIVER WILL USUALLY NOTICE THE `DESCRIPTION(S)` IN A **SUB-DOT**/**SUB-SECTION** UNDER THE **ORIGINAL** **DOT**/**SECTION** THAT CONTAINS THE `TAG` ITSELF.
2. WHEN THE PERCEIVER NOTICES **2 PAIRS** OF $PERCENT-SIGNS$ (_%.% AND %.%, **ONE** PAIR AT THE FRONT, **ONE** PAIR AT THE BACK_) ON THE ==OBSIDIAN SYSTEM==, IT WILL INDICATE THE START(S) AND END(S) OF THE `DESCRIPTION(S)` OF THE TAG ABOVE. THE 2 PAIRS OF $PERCENT-SIGNS$ WILL SERVE AS A **COMMENT**, IN THE VIEW OF THE ==OBSIDIAN SYSTEM==. IF IT (_THE `DESCRIPTION(S)`_) IS NOT VISIBLE, LET US KNOW, AND WE WILL TRY TO HELP THE BEST WAY(S) THAT WE SEE FIT.
3. THE `TAG(S)` ITSELF ARE USUALLY ABBREVIATION(S)/ACRONYM(S). IT MIGHT **APPEAR** AS RANDOM SEQUENCES OF LETTERS, BUT IT IS **TYPICALLY NOT** RANDOM, AND HAVE A MEANING OF **SOME** FORM. ONE OF THE ONLY SITUATIONS IN WHICH A `TAG` WILL HAVE TRULY RANDOM CHARACTERS WILL (_MOST LIKELY_) BE TO MAKE A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE ORIGINAL `TAG` (_NON-RANDOM SEQUENCE_) AND THE `TAG` IN QUESTION (_RANDOM SEQUENCE_), AS THEY MIGHT HAVE SIMILAR OR THE EXACT SAME ABBREVIATION(S)/ACRONYM(S).
4. ANOTHER INSTANCE IN WHICH THEY WILL HAVE TRULY RANDOM SEQUENCES OF CHARACTERS IS WHEN IT IS A CIPHER, OR SOME SORT OF SECRET INFORMATION. THIS COULD HAPPEN, SINCE CYBERSECURITY IS **NEVER** TOO REDUNDANT. BUT IF IT IS UNAVAILABLE TO VIEW, OR SEEMINGLY GIBBERISH, IT IS **PROBABLY FOR A REASON**.
5. THESE TAGS WILL OFTEN FORM A TREE-LIKE PATTERN, AND WILL HAVE DIFFERENT VARIANTS BETWEEN DIFFERENT AREAS, SIMILAR TO A RABBIT-HOLE. IF YOU WOULD LIKE TO SEARCH UP SPECIFIC `TAGS`, PLEASE TAKE INTO ACCOUNT THAT THEY MIGHT BE ABBREVIATED. IF YOU DO NOT KNOW THE `TAG` ABBREVIATION(S)/ACRONYM(S), SEARCH THROUGH THE FILES FULLY, WITH A FOCUS ON THE `TAG DESCRIPTION(S)` INSIDE THE 4 $PERCENT-SIGNS$(_2 FRONT, 2 BACK_). IF ALL ELSE FAILS, CONTACT US, AND **WE WILL HELP YOU THE BEST WAY(S) THAT WE SEE FIT**.
6. THE `TAG DESCRIPTION(S)` ITSELF WILL GO INTO **SOME** DETAIL. DETAILS IN THE `TAG DESCRIPTION(S)` **MAY INCLUDE, BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO**: USEFULNESS, ORGANIZATIONAL PURPOSE, WHAT THE `TAG` SEQUENCES STAND FOR (_IF IT STANDS FOR SOMETHING, THE MAJORITY WILL_), WHO TYPICALLY CONTRIBUTES TO THE `TAG` IN QUESTION (_AS WELL AS THE FILE_(S) _RELATED TO SAID `TAG`_), HOW OFTEN IT IS UPDATED, HOW LONG IT HAS BEEN SINCE IT WAS LAST UPDATED, WHERE THE `TAG` IS LOCATED, WHAT THE `TAG` IS CONNECTED TO, ETC.
7. KEEP IN MIND THAT THESE `TAGS` ARE MEANT TO BE USED ON THE ==OBSIDIAN SYSTEM==, SO THAT WE CAN ORGANIZE THINGS BETTER WHILE WE ARE DEVELOPING ASPECTS RELATED TO THESE PROJECTS. GITHUB USUALLY IS NOT THE MOST IDEAL PLACE FOR ONE TO SEARCH THESE `TAGS` UP AND THEIR `DESCRIPTION(S)`, SO USE THE TOOLS AT YOUR DISPOSAL CORRECTLY AND WELL.
8. ==OBSIDIAN SYSTEM== `TAGS` LIST WILL BE ALL LISTED IN THE [OBSIDIAN-TAG-MASTER-LIST.](obsidian://open?vault=PLASMICA&file=OBSIDIAN-TAGS-MASTER-LIST.)
